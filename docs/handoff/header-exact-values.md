### Where a value could not be the picture's (questions for the owner)

1. **Phone Week: the week cannot sit in the middle of the bar.** The picture's phone Week row is ◀ · week (flex 1) · ▶ 🖨 badge: one 52px button on the left and three on the right, so its own week sits between ◀ and ▶, 58px left of the bar's middle at 390.
